Sierra Leone

Sierra Leone was first sighted by Portuguese explorer Pedro de Sintra around 1462 during his voyage along the West African coast. He named the area "Serra Lyoa," meaning "Lion Mountains," because the mountains near the coast reminded him of lions or the sound of thunder over the hills. Over time, the name changed to Sierra Leone. Later, the country became an important center for trade and, in 1787, Freetown was established as a settlement for freed enslaved Africans. Sierra Leone gained its independence from Britain on 27 April 1961.

Traditional drums, dancing, and storytelling are important parts of Sierra Leonean culture. Modern genres such as Afrobeats, hip-hop, and reggae are also very popular.
