Mali

Mali is famous for its rich history and great discoveries. It was home to the powerful Mali Empire, one of the largest and wealthiest empires in West Africa. Mali became known for its large supplies of gold and salt, which were traded across Africa and beyond.

The city of Timbuktu became an important center of learning, where scholars studied religion, science, mathematics, and literature. Thousands of ancient manuscripts discovered in Timbuktu show that Mali was a leading center of education and knowledge during the Middle Ages.
