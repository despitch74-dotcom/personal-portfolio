Ghana

Ghana became the first country in sub-Saharan Africa to gain independence from British colonial rule, inspiring many other African countries to seek independence.Ghana discovered that its climate was excellent for growing cocoa and is now one of the world's largest cocoa producers.

Ghana has some of the world's richest gold reserves. Because of its large amount of gold, it was once called the Gold Coast by Europeans.Besides gold, Ghana has discovered and developed resources such as diamonds, bauxite, manganese, timber, and offshore oil and natural gas.
