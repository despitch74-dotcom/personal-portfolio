Guinea

Guinea is a country in West Africa. People lived there for thousands of years before Europeans arrived.In the 15th century, Portuguese explorers reached the coast of Guinea. They came to trade for gold, ivory, and other goods.

Guinea is also called the "Water Tower of West Africa." This is because the Niger, Senegal, and Gambia Rivers all begin in Guinea.
