Liberia

Liberia is a country in West Africa. It was founded in 1822 as a settlement for formerly enslaved African Americans who returned to Africa. In 1847, Liberia became Africa's first modern independent republic.

Liberia is rich in natural resources. Important discoveries include iron ore, gold, diamonds, rubber, and timber.The country also has beautiful forests and many different kinds of plants and animals.
